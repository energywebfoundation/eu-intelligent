# Address Book

## Overview

The Address Book provides a mechanism for mapping decentralized identifiers (DIDs) to human-readable alias names, enhancing usability and identification within the system.

***

## User Guide

### Pre-requisites

* Authorization to Client Gateway

### Add a DID to the Address Book

1. Navigate to `Address Book` page

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-07 at 11.16.30 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Address Book menu</p></figcaption></figure>

2. Click `Add`&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-07 at 11.21.27 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Add DID button</p></figcaption></figure>

3. Input `Alias` and `DID` in the form and click `Save`

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-06 at 11.24.54 AM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>Add DID form</p></figcaption></figure>

4. Success message will be shown, click `Dismiss` to close modal and view the address book

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (234).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>Successfully created contact</p></figcaption></figure>

### Modify a DID in the Address Book

1. Click on the `︙` button of the DID record that you wish to update, and choose `Update`&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-06 at 11.36.16 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Address book options</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (235).png" alt="" width="222"><figcaption><p>Update DID</p></figcaption></figure>

2. Modify `Alias` in the input field. Once done, click `Save`

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-06 at 11.42.11 AM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>Update DID form</p></figcaption></figure>

3. Success message will be shown, click `Dismiss` to close modal and view the address book

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (236).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>Successfully updated contact</p></figcaption></figure>

### Remove a DID in the Address Book

1. Click on the `︙` button of the DID record that you wish to update, and choose `Remove`&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-06 at 11.36.16 AM (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Address book options</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-06 at 11.39.35 AM.png" alt="" width="268"><figcaption><p>Remove DID</p></figcaption></figure>

2. A confirmation modal will appear, click `Confirm` to remove the DID from address book or `Cancel`&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (237).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>Delete DID confirmation modal</p></figcaption></figure>

3. Success message will be shown, click `Dismiss` to close modal and view the address book

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (238).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>Successfully deleted contact</p></figcaption></figure>
